# Third-party notices

Dependency inventories and retained license evidence belong to the app that ships them. Releases must include their platform's applicable notices.

- [Android third-party notices](apps/android/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md), including scanner SDK, cryptography components and runtime dependency evidence. The notice collector and bundled legal assets remain inside `apps/android`.
- Desktop notices will be imported with its source into `apps/desktop` and reviewed against its packaged dependencies before release.
